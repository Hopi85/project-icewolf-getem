ICEWOLF PORTABLE
================

Start:   double-click Icewolf.exe
         No installation, no administrator rights needed. Runs from a USB stick too.

Your data (bookmarks, history, settings) is stored in the "profile" folder
next to Icewolf.exe. Copy the whole Icewolf folder to take it with you.

UPDATES
After you close Icewolf, it checks for a new engine version (at most every 12 hours).
New versions are only installed if their checksum and digital signatures are valid.
Results: update-log.txt
To skip the update check once: Icewolf.exe /noupdate

PRIVACY
Icewolf Portable stores nothing on the PC except one registry value under
HKEY_CURRENT_USER\SOFTWARE\Mozilla\LibreWolf\TaskBarIDs. It holds the folder path
and is needed so Windows shows the Icewolf logo on the taskbar.
